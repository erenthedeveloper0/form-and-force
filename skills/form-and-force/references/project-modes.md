# Project modes

Different kinds of project need different priorities, a different key frame and a different idea of excellent. Pick the mode or modes that match and let them set the usual register, the budgets and what to verify. A mixed project (a marketing site with an application behind it) uses each mode on its own routes.

## 1. Mode selector

| Signal in the request or the repository | Mode | Usual register |
|---|---|---|
| "homepage", "brand", "rebrand", a company site | Brand and marketing | Composed to Expressive |
| A time-boxed launch, event, single-purpose microsite | Campaign | Expressive to Radical |
| Studio, agency, freelancer, personal site, "show my work" | Studio and portfolio | Expressive to Radical |
| Fashion, fragrance, jewellery, hospitality, high-end objects | Luxury | Expressive |
| Products, cart, checkout, catalogue, configurator | E-commerce | Expressive on story routes, Quiet on transactional ones |
| Pricing, features, a sign-up funnel for software | SaaS marketing | Composed |
| A logged-in product, settings, onboarding, workflows | SaaS application | Quiet to Composed |
| Tables, charts, filters, monitoring, admin | Dashboard and data | Quiet |
| Articles, essays, reports, long-form, "scrollytelling" | Editorial | Composed to Expressive |
| "Immersive", "WebGL", "3D", "generative", "experimental" | Experimental | Radical |
| Tokens, components, "design system", "UI kit" | Design system | Quiet to Composed |
| An existing site or app to improve | Redesign, plus the underlying mode | The underlying mode's |
| A local firm, clinic, trade, public body, "simple and clear" | Restrained business | Quiet to Composed |
| "Explore", "prototype", "concepts" | Prototype | Any |

The register in the table is where to start. The brief decides ([direction.md](direction.md) §3).

## 2. Brand and marketing site
- **Excellent means** a visitor understands what this is, why it is different and what to do next, said in a way only this organisation could say it.
- **Key frame:** the first screen.
- **Priorities:** the position, then the hierarchy of content, proof, the path to action, craft.
- **Structure:** from the visitor's questions (what is it, is it for me, can I trust it, what does it cost, what now), in the order they are really asked.
- **Motion layers:** feedback, continuity, one composed arrival, one signature moment.
- **The twin:** a centred hero, a logo strip, three feature cards, testimonials, a closing call to action.
- **Keep convention:** findable navigation, a visible primary action, contact, a legal footer.
- **Verify:** the twin test with a competitor's name; a five-second recall of the offer; LCP on a phone.

## 3. Campaign, microsite and launch
- **Excellent means** one memorable idea carried all the way through, fast enough to share on a phone.
- **Key frame:** the moment the idea is first understood. It may not be the first screen.
- **Priorities:** the signature moment, shareability (preview images, deep links), load speed, a plan for the site's end of life.
- **Motion layers:** all five may be used, with a strict budget on phones and a reduced-motion version that still tells the story.
- **Gates** need a reason, a skip and keyboard access.
- **Lifespan:** campaign sites decay. Plan a static archive and a sunset date.
- **The twin:** a dark page, a large object rotating, a word-by-word statement.
- **Verify:** time to meaningful content on a mid-range phone; share previews; sound muted by default.

## 4. Studio, agency and portfolio
- **Excellent means** the work is the hero, and the site itself shows how this studio thinks.
- **Key frame:** the index of work, or the first screen if the studio leads with a position.
- **Priorities:** depth in each case (problem, approach, outcome), curation, moving between projects, contact.
- **Signature:** one authored mechanic that proves the studio's own craft. A borrowed format proves someone else's.
- **The twin:** a minimal white grid of thumbnails with video on hover; or a dark page with an "Enter" gate and a custom cursor.
- **Keep convention:** a fast, complete index; a direct link to every project; readable text.
- **Verify:** can someone on a phone reach a relevant case in two actions? Does each case state its outcome?

## 5. Luxury and premium brand
- **Excellent means** desire built from specifics: the object, the material, the hand that made it, the place. Confidence shows in what is left out.
- **Key frame:** the object or the world at its most exact.
- **Priorities:** photography and its direction, typography with a voice, pacing with real rests, exactness at every edge.
- **Premium is not a palette.** Cream grounds, a thin serif and slow fades are one convention among many, and the most copied. Take colour, type and tempo from this house: its materials, its archive, its place, its temperament. A house can be loud, cold, playful, severe or strange and still be luxury.
- **Motion layers:** feedback and continuity that are immaculate; arrivals with the house's own tempo; at most one signature moment.
- **The twin:** a full-bleed photograph, a centred serif wordmark, "Discover the collection".
- **Keep convention:** the route to a product and to purchase is never obscured; prices and availability are honest.
- **Verify:** the cold read names an attitude specific to this house; the phone composition is directed, not stacked.

## 6. E-commerce and product storytelling
- **Excellent means** the product is unmistakable and buying it is effortless.
- **Split the experience.** Story routes (launch, collection, editorial) may be Expressive. Transactional routes (listing filters, the buy box, cart, checkout, account) are Quiet: conventional, fast and robust.
- **Key frame:** the product detail page's first screen, and one story scene.
- **Product page essentials:** price, variants, stock, delivery and returns, size help, real imagery at full fidelity, a persistent add-to-cart on phones, reviews only if real.
- **The twin:** a hero carousel, a grid of identical cards, a hover-only second image.
- **Never:** fake scarcity, invented reviews, key images hidden in a carousel.
- **Verify:** add-to-cart and checkout end to end (a bounded mock if there is no back end), error and out-of-stock states, input delay on variant switching.

## 7. SaaS marketing site
- **Excellent means** the product's real mechanism is shown with real interface, and the buyer can qualify themselves.
- **Key frame:** the first screen with the product doing its job.
- **Proof:** named customers with permission, specific outcomes with sources, security and compliance facts.
- **Pricing:** compared by the job to be done; honest limits; an enterprise route; questions taken from real objections.
- **The twin:** a headline over a gradient, a screenshot in a browser frame, three feature cards with icons, "trusted by" logos, a pricing table.
- **Verify:** the twin test with a direct competitor's name; every number traced to a source.

## 8. SaaS and web application
- **Excellent means** the person reaches value quickly and every state is handled. Delight lives in feedback and flow.
- **Key frame:** the main working screen at real density, with real or labelled sample data.
- **Priorities:** information architecture, task flows, states (empty, loading, error, partial, permission), keyboard efficiency, consistency, character in small moments.
- **Motion layers:** feedback and continuity only. Nothing triggered from the keyboard animates. 80 to 250 ms.
- **The device** usually lives in the interaction model, the shape of the data and the typography of numbers and labels.
- **The twin:** the component library's demo: a sidebar, a top bar, cards with soft shadows.
- **Verify:** state inventories complete; tasks completed by keyboard alone; a second screen that is consistent with the first; input delay under realistic data sizes.

## 9. Dashboard, admin and data-heavy interface
- **Excellent means** the right number is findable, trustworthy and actionable, at a glance and on drilling down.
- **Key frame:** the screen an operator looks at most, with realistic data including the awkward cases.
- **Priorities:** relationships and comparisons in the data; labels and units; visible time context; filters that filter; geometry that stays still while data loads.
- **Motion layers:** feedback, and continuity that preserves object constancy (bars that change length, rows that reorder). No arrival animation on every load.
- **Charts** are chosen by task (compare, trend, distribution, composition, relationship), labelled directly, encoded redundantly, with a table alternative.
- **The twin:** a row of metric cards with sparklines, neon charts on black, a "live" pulse.
- **Verify:** realistic volumes (thousands of rows, virtualised); empty and error panels; keyboard movement through grids; number formatting and locale.

## 10. Editorial, publication and long-form story
- **Excellent means** reading is a pleasure, the argument is clear and the visuals carry evidence.
- **Key frame:** the opening spread, and one screen of plain reading text. Both must be good.
- **Priorities:** measure, leading and hierarchy; pacing; visuals in step with the text; navigation inside long pieces; shareable deep links.
- **Scroll stories:** text leads and graphics follow. Every step is reachable by keyboard and by link. A static version tells the same story.
- **Motion layers:** narrative inside the story's graphics; almost nothing in the reading chrome.
- **The twin:** a parallax hero photograph, paragraphs that fade up, a drop cap.
- **Verify:** a measure of 45 to 75 characters; reading one-handed on a phone; the reduced-motion story intact; an acceptable reader or print view.

## 11. Experimental, immersive and generative
- **Excellent means** a world with its own rules that people understand within seconds, built with production discipline.
- **Key frame:** the first five seconds: orientation and the first action.
- **Priorities:** the idea, the first five seconds, the mechanic, performance on target devices, a parallel accessible layer with the same content, a reduced-motion version.
- **Engineering:** device tiers and quality scaling; loops paused when hidden; resources disposed; sound opt-in; a poster frame; a no-WebGL fallback ([creative-coding.md](creative-coding.md)).
- **The twin:** particles that form a logo; an exploded product render; a neon grid; a blob that follows the pointer.
- **Verify:** in a headed browser and on a real mid-range phone (headless GPUs stall); stable memory across navigation; frame time; the fallback path.

## 12. Design system and component library
- **Excellent means** the system makes the right thing easy and allows character without forking.
- **Key frame:** a specimen page that uses the system to document itself.
- **Priorities:** token architecture (primitive, role, component); accessible primitives with documented states; composition patterns; generated motion tokens; theming; documentation that demonstrates.
- **The twin:** renamed library defaults, one radius and one shadow, pages of static swatches.
- **Verify:** every component in every state; keyboard support; right-to-left and long text; dark mode; reduced motion; a visual regression baseline.

## 13. Redesign or audit of an existing product
- **Excellent means** measurable improvement that respects what works. Never wipe out working architecture, addresses or content.
- **Process:**
  1. Baseline: `inspect_repo.mjs`, `slop_scan.mjs` and `audit_page.mjs` on the current site. Keep the evidence.
  2. Diagnose with the five failure classes ([floor.md](floor.md) §4) and a cold read of the current first screen ([jury.md](jury.md) §4).
  3. Separate floor fixes (do them now) from changes of direction (need the hand).
  4. Mine the existing equity. Owned marks, colours and typefaces are binding.
  5. Put the current page on the sketch board beside the new frames.
  6. Migrate in steps.
- **If the user asked only for an audit or for fixes,** do that. Report direction-level findings as findings; do not redesign uninvited.
- **Keep:** addresses or redirects, search metadata, analytics events, content models, accessible behaviour that already works.
- **Verify:** before and after boards, before and after facts, no lost routes, no regressions in existing tests.

## 14. Restrained business and service sites
- **Excellent means** a person finds what they need at once and leaves trusting the organisation.
- **Key frame:** the first screen with the three things people actually come for.
- **Restraint is the brief, and it is not the same as blandness.** The Quiet register asks for one conviction held without exception: a typographic rule, a way of ordering, a tone of plain speech, an exact fact where others have a slogan.
- **Priorities:** the task (hours, prices, contact, booking, eligibility); plain language; speed; accessibility well beyond the minimum.
- **Do not** add an effect, a concept or a scroll sequence to make it more impressive. Overwrought is a failure here.
- **The twin:** a stock photograph, "Welcome to", three service cards, a contact form.
- **Verify:** the main task completed on a phone in under a minute; the page readable at 200% zoom; nothing invented.

## 15. Prototype and exploration
- **Excellent means** a design question answered quickly and honestly.
- **Rules:** label it as a prototype; mark sample data; build only the paths that answer the question; record the question and the answer.
- **Allowed:** stubbed back ends behind an explicit demo label. **Not allowed:** passing a prototype off as finished, or leaving placeholder media in anything shipped.
