# Templates

Five documents the agent fills in, in [`skills/form-and-force/assets/`](../../skills/form-and-force/assets/). Each is short on purpose: a template that takes an hour to fill becomes a ritual, and its content stops being checked.

| Template | Written | Read by |
|---|---|---|
| [Direction brief](#direction-brief) | After choosing a frame | Every later decision, and the jury, last |
| [Motion score](#motion-score) | Before coding any sequence of two or more movers | The build, and the jury |
| [Component spec](#component-spec) | Before building a custom component | The build and its tests |
| [Jury brief](#jury-brief) | Given to a juror in a fresh context | The juror |
| [Handoff report](#handoff-report) | At the end | You |

## Direction brief

[`direction-brief.md`](../../skills/form-and-force/assets/direction-brief.md). One screen of text: the mode and scope, the register and why, the position, the device and what it forbids, the chosen frame and the runner-up, **the twin it has to beat**, the facts the direction stands on, the language extracted from the frame (composition, type, colour, imagery, motion character), the truth inventory, and the budgets. It is the reference every later decision is checked against.

## Motion score

[`motion-score.md`](../../skills/form-and-force/assets/motion-score.md). The motion character and the tokens generated from it, the layers in use and what each contains, the gate's decisions (including what deliberately does not move), each sequence cast and timed, the life of each designed interaction from rest to reduced motion, the signature moment if there is one, and what was actually seen in a filmstrip.

## Component spec

[`component-spec.md`](../../skills/form-and-force/assets/component-spec.md). For a custom component: its purpose, how it carries the project's device (or which convention it keeps and why), its anatomy, content schema, props, states and transitions, interaction model for pointer, touch, keyboard and assistive technology, motion per state with its reduced version, responsive variants, accessibility semantics, error and empty states, integrations and testing strategy; then a state inventory (designed, built, tested) and a typed contract.

## Jury brief

[`jury-brief.md`](../../skills/form-and-force/assets/jury-brief.md). What a juror in a fresh context receives with the evidence, and nothing else until the cold read is written: the cold read from the images only, the facts and filmstrips, then the direction brief, the ten dimensions, findings in five parts, and the four verdicts. It keeps the person who wrote the rationale out of the judgement.

## Handoff report

[`report-template.md`](../../skills/form-and-force/assets/report-template.md). The direction and the twin it had to beat, what changed, the floor with **actual results only**, the jury's marks with evidence and its verdict, what is open, and what needs your decision or input. A check that did not run is reported as not run.
