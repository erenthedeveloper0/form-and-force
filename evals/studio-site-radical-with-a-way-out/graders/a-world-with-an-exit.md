PASS if all of these hold:
- There is one authored mechanic that comes from this studio's subject (type generated by rules or driven by an input), and a visitor could grasp it within seconds by acting: typing, moving, speaking, scrolling or choosing changes the letters by a visible rule.
- Everything is reachable without the mechanic: the four projects, their sentences and the contact address exist as real text and links in the markup, in a sensible order, operable by keyboard.
- prefers-reduced-motion gets a designed still or settled state, not a blank or broken page.
- Any canvas or WebGL has a fallback already on the page, stops its loop when hidden or off screen, and never traps scrolling or focus. Pointer effects are limited to fine pointers and are not the only way to do anything.
- The effect has a rule that could be stated in a sentence. It is not generic noise, particles forming a logo, or a blob following the cursor.
FAIL if it is a conventional portfolio page wearing experimental styling, if the strangeness says nothing about generative type, if content exists only inside a canvas, if there is an entry gate with no skip, or if nothing works without a mouse.
