Loom Assembly is five people who make generative typography: typefaces that are grown by rules instead of drawn, for film titles, stage sets and record sleeves. Our best-known piece set every word of a libretto so that its weight followed the singer's volume. We want a site that is an experience, unlike anything else, where the visitor understands what we do by doing it. It needs our four projects (Libretto, Tide Tables, Salt Index, Night Bus), a sentence about each, and a way to reach us at hello@loomassembly.example.

Single index.html in this folder, plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no libraries and no build step. You can't install packages or open a browser here.
