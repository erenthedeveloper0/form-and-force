PASS if all of these hold:
- Running text is set for reading: a measure of roughly 45 to 75 characters, body size of at least 16px with leading around 1.4 to 1.6, real punctuation, and a narrow-screen setting with its own decisions.
- The opening is a designed spread in which the title, standfirst and byline have a clear order, and the typography has a voice suited to this subject. A screen of plain body text is as considered as the opening.
- The six yearly figures are shown as a graphic drawn for this data (in SVG, HTML or CSS), with units and years, true to the numbers supplied, and with a table or text equivalent for assistive technology.
- The text leads: nothing fades or slides in as paragraphs scroll into view, there is no parallax, and any motion sits inside the graphic and has a reduced-motion version.
- The place where the remaining text will go is clearly marked as such, and no further facts, quotes or figures are invented.
FAIL if paragraphs animate on scroll, if the figures are decoration with no labels, if any figure is altered or added, if body text runs the full width of a wide screen, or if a drop cap and a parallax photograph are the whole of the design.
