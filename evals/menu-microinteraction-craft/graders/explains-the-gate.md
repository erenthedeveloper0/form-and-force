PASS if the final message explains the changes by how often the menu is used and by what the person is doing: something opened dozens of times a day, often from the keyboard, gets little or no animation, and what remains exists to confirm the action. It should list what was removed as well as what was changed, and say that the result was not seen in a browser.
FAIL if it presents added animation as the improvement, or claims to have watched or measured the motion.
