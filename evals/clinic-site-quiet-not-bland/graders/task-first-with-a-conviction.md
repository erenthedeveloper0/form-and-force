PASS if all of these hold:
- Booking, the two prices with their durations, and the address with opening hours are all on or immediately reachable from the first screen, in plain words, with the phone number as a real tel: link and the form made of labelled native controls.
- The page holds one visible conviction without exception: a typographic rule, an ordering principle, or exact facts set where others would put a slogan. It reads as designed for these patients (generous text size, strong contrast, large targets), not as a component demo.
- There is no decorative motion: no entrance animation, scroll effect, carousel or hover flourish. Feedback on controls is immediate. Focus is visible.
- Nothing is invented: no testimonials, ratings, staff biographies, qualifications or patient counts that were not supplied.
FAIL if it is overwrought for this brief (a hero effect, gradients and glows, animation on scroll, a concept that needs explaining). FAIL equally if it is bland by default: a stock-photo hero or its placeholder, "Welcome to", three service cards and a contact form, with nothing that shows a decision.
