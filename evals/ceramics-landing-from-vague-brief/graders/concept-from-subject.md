PASS if the page's structure and visual decisions clearly derive from this studio's specifics: wood firing, glaze variation, one-of-one pieces, or firing-based drops. For example, products are presented as unique pieces from a named firing, drop timing is part of the flow, or the materials and imagery strategy reference the kiln. PASS also requires that any missing proof (reviews, press, customer counts) is absent or shown as a clearly labelled placeholder rather than invented.
FAIL if the page is a generic template (centred hero with two buttons, three icon feature cards, testimonial carousel) that would fit any brand after swapping the name, or if it contains invented testimonials, ratings, press logos or statistics.
