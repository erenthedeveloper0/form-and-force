We are Vessel & Sons, knife makers in Sheffield since 1921, four generations in the same forge on Garden Street. Every kitchen knife is forged by hand from carbon steel and takes eleven days: forged, normalised three times, ground, quenched in oil, tempered twice, handled in bog oak. We leave the forge scale on the flats, black, with the hammer marks in it. We are not a quiet brand: the workshop is loud, hot and filthy, and we are proud of that. Knives cost from £480 to £2,100 and we make about 300 a year.

Make our homepage. It has to feel like the most desirable knives in the world, and it must not look like every other luxury site. Single index.html in this folder, no build step. The photo shoot is next month, so there are no pictures yet. You can't install packages or open a browser here.
