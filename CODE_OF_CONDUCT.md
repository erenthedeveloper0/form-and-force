# Code of conduct

Form & Force is a small project about making work for other people with care. The way its contributors treat each other should meet the same standard.

## Expected

- Be specific and kind when you critique. The method's own rule applies to people too: a useful comment names where, what is seen, why it matters and what would change it. "This is bad" is not a finding.
- Assume good faith. Contributors write in many first languages and with many levels of experience, including people opening their first pull request.
- Credit what you take: sources in the research, people in the changelog.
- Say plainly what you did not check. An honest "not verified" is worth more than an optimistic summary.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, body or ability.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information, including details from their projects, without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Pressuring anyone to work unpaid to a deadline, or to share credentials.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems to the maintainer, Eren Sümer (erenthedeveloper0 on GitHub). To report privately, open an issue titled "Conduct report: please contact me" with no details, and the maintainer will arrange a private conversation. Reports are handled in confidence, and no one is penalised for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and warn, suspend or block participants, in proportion to what happened and with a reason given where it is safe to give one. These decisions are the maintainer's, and they are recorded privately.
